Calculus

    Introduction

    This website is an attempt to build one collective resource for learning introductory calculus at a high school and undergraduate level.

    Problems Note: All the practice problems are designed to be dificult for your benefit, however, there is no gain from getting frustrated and staring at a problem for an hour. So, I have included hints beneath challenge problems that have been currated to still allow you to come to the answer without being told what to do. But, as Jeff Dunworth would say, there is no gain from solving problems that don't require some head scratching.

    Quality Note: This learning tool was fully developed by one individual and will contain typos, grammatically incorrect sentences, and sometimes questions that aren't worded properly. Furthermore, this website will attempt to be inclusive of everything you need to learn calculus but I can't gaurentee every topic you wish to see will be covered. (Formatted around AP Calculus AB - this website has no affiliation to college board). On the other hand, you may find the amount of material excessive for some topics, this comes from my own personal use of calculus and what topics I believe are most important for your success and setting you up to do well in higher level math.

    Resources Note: For simplicity and ease of development, images won't be labeled with sources. Instead, I will list all of sources used throughout this project as a whole: [[[SOURCES NEED TO BE ADDED AS YOU USE THEM]]]

    AI Note: No artificial intelligence was used for the production of any code (with the exception of the CSS file), content, problem, answer, or idea present on this website. However, grammar, sentence structure, and answers to problems were checked with ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude to double check the quality of problems and to keep the reading standard acceptable as I don't have the time to thoroughly check every problem. Any mistakes found with large language models will be represented under "tricky mistakes" in the hint section of problems.
